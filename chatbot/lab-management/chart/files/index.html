Auto-approval

FAIG requires an administrator to approve every detector, guardrail and policy a student submits or deletes. When this is on, the lab approves every pending change about once a minute, using your signed-in FAIG session. It keeps running when you close this page, and stops when the time below runs out, when your FAIG session ends (at most 8 hours after you signed in), when you turn it off or sign out, or when the lab gateway restarts. Opening this page restarts it if it is on.

Prepopulate existing students

For students that already exist with a stored password. Creates what is missing for the selected scenarios; existing objects and keys are kept. Each layer is approved with your session.



Reset a student's password

Sets a new FAIG password, stores it for the share page and checks it by signing in as the student. If FAIG refuses or the new password does not work, the stored password is kept.
